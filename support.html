Rectoly — Support

Last updated August 19, 2026

Hawk Eye develops Rectoly, an iPad app for reading, annotating, and syncing academic PDFs with reference managers including Mendeley Reference Manager and Zotero. Use this page to get help with the app.

Contact

For support questions, bug reports, or feedback, email us at rectoly.app@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iPadOS version, and Rectoly app version when reporting a problem.

Subscriptions and billing

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device, or manage it at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
